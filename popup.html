Screen Recorder

Professional screen recording
How it works
  1. Click "Start Recording" above
  2. Select screen, window or tab to record
  3. Recorder minimizes automatically
  4. Do your recording
  5. Click recorder in taskbar to stop
💡
To stop: click the recorder window in your taskbar, or press Ctrl+Shift+S. Recording stops instantly.
